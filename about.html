Our History

A Church-Driven Mission

Operating under the spiritual coverage of the Anglican Church of Kenya (ACK) Budokomi Church, our center serves as a beacon of hope. In collaborative partnership with Compassion International Kenya, we act as a local implementer for holistic child development.

We firmly believe that poverty is multi-dimensional. By intervening early in a child’s life, we can break generational cycles and establish lasting transformation through the local church.

Our Identity

Compassion Core Values

At ACK Budokomi CYDC, our staff, mentors, and partners live out these six core values every single day to serve our children:

✝

Integrity

We commit to the highest standard of personal and professional integrity, ensuring transparency and honesty in all our operations.

💙

Discernment

We seek God's wisdom and guidance in managing resources and making choices that best serve the vulnerable youth in Budokomi.

🤝

Dignity

We respect and value every child, family member, and partner as unique individuals made in the image of God.

🌱

Stewardship

We treat every resource as a trust from God, managing funds and supplies efficiently to maximize community impact.

✨

Excellence

We strive for excellence in our programs, education, health care, and child protection tracking systems.

⛪

Partnership

We honor our collaboration with ACK Budokomi Church and local families to achieve sustainable, church-driven development.

The Promise

Our Guarding Commitments

We hold ourselves to strict criteria verified by local and global ministry frameworks to secure safety and measurable development progress.

✓ Known: Every registered child is tracked individually to verify their medical, academic, and economic challenges are directly met.
✓ Loved: We provide structured personal mentorship, spiritual discipleship, and a loving communal environment.
✓ Protected: We implement rigorous child safeguarding frameworks to shield children from abuse, neglect, and exploitation.
